# Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR)

Here are my investigation notes from Hack The Box Sherlocks, LetsDefend cases, and memory/disk forensics exercises—focused on reconstructing attack timelines, analyzing artifacts, and identifying Indicators of Compromise (IOCs).

## Lab Setup
For forensic investigations and incident triage, I use a dedicated **Windows Forensics VM** alongside **REMnux** for Linux/network-based analysis, equipped with standard DFIR tools including:
- **Windows Artifacts & Triage:** KAPE, Eric Zimmerman's Tools (Timeline Explorer, Registry Explorer, EvtxECmd, PECmd)
- **Memory & Disk Forensics:** Volatility 3, Autopsy, FTK Imager
- **Network & Log Analysis:** Wireshark, NetworkMiner, Splunk / Elastic SIEM
